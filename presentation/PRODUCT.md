## Stack

## Users

## Product Purpose
Explain why IoT integrity matters and demonstrate the proposed CyberScope onboarding and runtime-attestation flow.

## Operating Context
Two-slide preview: Problem Statement and Design & Methodology. The eventual deck adds a title, demo and thank-you slide. Speaker notes preserve the supplied narration.

## Capabilities and Constraints
Diagram depicts proposed architecture, not a claim of completed integration. SHA-256 is hashing, not encryption. Sensor data is separate from device parameters. A matching hash indicates unchanged monitored state, not proof of the absence of compromise. Animation loops over onboarding, acceptance, USB tampering, rejection, alerting and approved restoration.

## Brand Commitments
Bold editorial presentation. Warm ivory, near-black, cobalt, teal acceptance and vermilion rejection. Oversized headings and statistics; precise vector diagrams. Manual slide navigation and a readable 30-second diagram loop.

## Evidence on Hand
